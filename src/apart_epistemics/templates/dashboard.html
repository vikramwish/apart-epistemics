What do these metrics mean?
Accuracy
Fraction of correct ground-truth answers; higher is better.
Brier
Squared probability error. Lower Brier is better; confident errors are penalized.
Confident wrong
Rate of confident but incorrect answers; lower is better.
Effective topics
Hill N1: topic diversity expressed as an effective number of equally common topics.
Goldman N_eff
Effective independent sources; correlated sources reduce this value.
Jaccard distance
Lexical difference between responses, not a measure of factual accuracy.
